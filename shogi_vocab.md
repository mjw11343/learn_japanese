| Vocabulary | Literal Meaning | Kanji Components | Construction | Memory Aid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **King / Challenger**<br>王将 / 玉将<br>(*ōshō / gyokushō*) | King General /<br>Jeweled General | **王** (*ō*): king<br>*Learning tip: The three horizontal lines represent heaven, man, and earth, united by the vertical line of the king.*<br><br>**玉** (*gyoku*): jewel<br>*Learning tip: A king holding a precious jewel (the dot). In Shogi, the reigning champion gets the King (王), while the challenger gets the Jewel (玉).*<br><br>**将** (*shō*): commander / general<br>*Learning tip: Shows a hand (爪) and a thumb/measurement (寸) next to a wooden command desk (爿). Represents a commander directing troops.* | **王 / 玉**: Ideographs (指事文字).<br><br>**将**: Phono-semantic compound (形声文字). | The reigning **King** (王) and the challenger's **Jewel** (玉) rule the board, commanding as the top **General** (将). |
| **Rook**<br>飛車<br>(*hisha*) | Flying Chariot | **飛** (*hi*): fly<br>*Learning tip: A complex pictograph of a bird in flight. Look for the wings (⺃) sweeping upward on the right side.*<br><br>**車** (*sha*): car / chariot<br>*Learning tip: A top-down view of a traditional two-wheeled chariot, with an axle running through the square cabin in the middle.* | **飛**: Pictograph (象形文字).<br><br>**車**: Pictograph (象形文字). | The Rook zooms across the board so fast it is like a **chariot** (車) taking to the sky to **fly** (飛). |
| **Bishop**<br>角行<br>(*kakugyō*) | Angle Mover | **角** (*kaku*): angle / horn<br>*Learning tip: Originally a pictograph of an animal horn with horizontal ridges. Because horns point outward, it evolved to mean "angle" or "corner."*<br><br>**行** (*gyō*): go / move<br>*Learning tip: Depicts a four-way crossroad or intersection, indicating movement or a journey.* | **角**: Pictograph / Ideograph.<br><br>**行**: Pictograph (象形文字). | The Bishop can only **move** (行) along the diagonals or **angles** (角) of the board. |
| **Gold General**<br>金将<br>(*kinshō*) | Gold General | **金** (*kin*): gold / metal<br>*Learning tip: The top umbrella shape represents the earth, while the strokes underneath are nuggets of gold buried deep in the soil.*<br><br>**将** (*shō*): commander / general | **金**: Ideograph (指事文字).<br><br>**将**: Phono-semantic compound. | The highly valued **Gold** (金) **General** (将) stays close to the King to protect his wealth and life. |
| **Silver General**<br>銀将<br>(*ginshō*) | Silver General | **銀** (*gin*): silver<br>- **金** (*kanehen*): metal radical.<br>*Learning tip: Compressed on the left, this signals the kanji is a metal/mineral.*<br>- **艮** (*gon*): tough / stopping.<br>*Learning tip: Provides the "gin" sound and the concept of something hard or unyielding.*<br><br>**将** (*shō*): commander / general | **銀**: Phono-semantic compound (形声文字). The left (金) gives the category, the right (艮) gives the sound.<br><br>**将**: Phono-semantic compound. | The **Silver** (銀) metal is tough and unyielding, making an excellent frontline **General** (将) for attack. |
| **Knight**<br>桂馬<br>(*keima*) | Cassia Horse | **桂** (*kei*): katsura tree / cassia<br>- **木** (*kihen*): tree radical.<br>*Learning tip: Placed on the left, this always hints at plants or wood.*<br>- **圭** (*kei*): squared jewel.<br><br>**馬** (*ma*): horse<br>*Learning tip: A pictograph of a horse standing on its hind legs. The top lines are the mane, and the four bottom dots (灬) are its four legs.* | **桂**: Phono-semantic compound. Left gives meaning (tree), right gives sound (*kei*).<br><br>**馬**: Pictograph (象形文字). | Picture a **horse** (馬) jumping gracefully over the branches of a sweet-smelling **cassia tree** (桂). |
| **Lance**<br>香車<br>(*kyōsha*) | Incense Chariot | **香** (*kyō*): incense / fragrance<br>- **禾** (*nogihen*): grain.<br>- **曰** (sun / sweet).<br>*Learning tip: Depicts the sweet, fragrant smell of millet or grain cooking over a fire.*<br><br>**車** (*sha*): car / chariot | **香**: Compound ideograph (会意文字). Combines two meanings (sweet + grain) to make fragrance.<br><br>**車**: Pictograph (象形文字). | The Lance charges straight ahead like a **chariot** (車), leaving a straight trail of burning **incense** (香) smoke behind it. |
| **Pawn**<br>歩兵<br>(*fuhyō*) | Foot Soldier | **歩** (*fu*): walk / step<br>- **止** (*tome*): stop / foot.<br>- **少** (*suko*): a little.<br>*Learning tip: Originally depicted a left foot and a right foot taking one step forward.*<br><br>**兵** (*hyō*): soldier<br>*Learning tip: Depicts a pair of hands (the bottom 八) gripping an axe or weapon (斤), representing a soldier.* | **歩**: Compound ideograph (会意文字).<br><br>**兵**: Compound ideograph (会意文字). | The humble **soldier** (兵) gripping his weapon, advancing exactly one **step** (歩) at a time. |
